## Standards

**P2, closed: ingestion evidence was not preserved or displayed consistently.** Ingestion writes `evidence_json`, while relationship merging and inspection handled only `evidence_spans`. Merging two identities with distinct same-document quotes could lose a quote; the inspector could claim no quote was recorded when one existed. This violated the supporting-quote requirement in `docs/specs/accuracy-and-reliability.md`.

Storage now combines both fields into deduplicated per-document `evidence_spans` before merging. Multi-document edges keep quotes attached to their source records. The inspector accepts nested legacy records and scalar `source_doc` records, reads both evidence fields, and deduplicates visible quotes. Existing source text remains escaped.

A real Neo4j create/merge/read regression failed against the immutable pre-fix `56baf7c` snapshot and passed after correction. A frontend regression covers scalar, nested and mixed formats. The browser fixture now uses ingestion's actual `evidence_json` format. The follow-up review found no remaining material standards finding.
