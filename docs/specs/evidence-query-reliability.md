## Problem and intended outcome

The exact question is “how has my insurance changed over the years and what is the most current”, in timeline mode. The saved request took 179.3 seconds, found 90 items from 17 documents, audited 12 claims, and withheld the entire answer because two chosen references lacked the precise date values. The needed full-year homeowners dates were in the supplied source span. An indexed older homeowners policy was absent from retrieval. Timeline rejected both proposed events; their original rejection causes were not persisted.

The intended behavior is a useful source-backed answer across document domains: explain recorded changes over time, identify the latest documented observations for each relevant subject, and state unresolved gaps with source links. Insurance policies are one representative acceptance case, alongside bills, contractual terms and medical measurements. It must not require the user to narrow an ordinary question just to work around citation formatting. It must not invent active coverage, current balances or conditions, submission events, supersession, century or precise dates. When only part can be verified, return the independently reverified portion with a clear completeness limitation instead of discarding it or exposing an unsupported draft.

This is a query-layer reliability change, not an insurance-specific rule set. No policy-specific identifiers, insurer names, years, question strings, or fixed policy-family classifier may control acceptance or retrieval. Use existing domain metadata and query-derived subject/type hints only as retrieval signals. Tests must demonstrate the same contracts in at least two unrelated domains, including changing bills/contract terms and dated medical measurements.

The [investigation](../audits/2026-09-08-insurance-history-query.md) distinguishes exact-request reproductions from related controlled defects. All private OCR, identifiers, captured payloads and source checks stay outside GitHub. Synthetic fixtures must exercise the same paths without copying private content.

## D1 and D2: precise dates and useful repair

Create one small source-date module used by answer values and timeline validation. Represent a recognized calendar occurrence with canonical ISO value, precision and original character range. Parse calendar-valid ISO year/month/day, English full/abbreviated month forms and slash dates under an explicit source numeric-date-order setting. Default to month/day/year for this US document archive, document the convention, support day/month/year and reject-ambiguous settings, and include this setting in query cache identity. Never silently infer a different order from the proposed answer. A two-digit year cannot supply a century by itself; use an explicit full-year supporting date or retain source precision rather than pivoting around today's year. Compact unlabelled digit strings are identifiers, not dates.

Numeric verification may consume equivalent date occurrences only after date support is established. Remaining amounts, signs, units, policy identifiers and factual heading numbers still use the existing exact-value checks. Do not join separate quotes to fabricate adjacency or a date. Exact reference membership, raw OCR certification, source digest and offsets remain mandatory. Semantic event meaning remains separately audited; a date comparison does not prove submission, cancellation or a policy change.

Expose bounded structured value mismatch details on each rejected claim, including missing date/value/unit categories. Send those details to the existing answer repairer. Update audit/repair instructions to cite every asserted value and date, include a full-year passage where available, and phrase a record as dated rather than submitted when no submission evidence exists. Do not add an output-token cap or unbounded repair loop. Keep original plus one repair; a partial-subset audit is a separate bounded validation step with no new generation.

## A1 and A2: partial delivery contract

Full supported/qualified answers retain their existing meaning. Add a `partial` disposition for a newly verified subset, distinct from answer completeness. `finalization.complete` remains false for partial; add an explicit delivered-answer verification flag. The delivered claim ledger is complete for exactly the text shown and has its own candidate digest and correct offsets. Preserve the failed candidate's digest, total/supported/omitted counts and bounded omission reasons separately as diagnostics; never attach its ledger to the new text. User-visible gap summaries describe missing verification and count, not unsupported facts presented as conclusions.

Subset eligibility requires a fully audited original or repaired candidate, at least one supported unit and at least one unsupported unit, no conflicting or unchecked units, no malformed ledger/invalid attribution, and a stable source snapshot. Never derive a partial answer from timeout, unavailable audit, over-limit/incomplete audit, corpus_changed or unaudited quick mode. Use only the final attempted candidate when that final attempt itself completed a full eligible audit. Never fall back to an earlier fully audited candidate after repair timeout, failed second audit or incomplete replacement. Add the explicit negative regression: complete initial audit followed by failed/incomplete repair must still withhold the answer. Do not silently split a factual unit or remove a negation/condition inside it.

Assemble whole supported units in original order, then invoke the ordinary source auditor against the same full evidence pack with only that subset as answer context. Recompute units, references, source selection, all numeric checks, temporal acceptance and whole-answer validation. Missing antecedents, contradictions and unsupported implied completeness must fail this audit. If it does not wholly pass, keep the ordinary refusal. No recursive subset attempt and no third generative rewrite.

Display a plain “Verified partial answer” notice, supported-delivered-claim coverage, original omitted count, and a clear statement that the answer is incomplete. A nonzero verification indicator reflects the audited text being shown, not the original provider confidence or complete archive coverage. Preserve source detail, restored history, model/mode display and responsive UI. Do not cache partial results as complete successes; a new request must have the opportunity to recover omitted facts. Corpus mutation checks clear all delivered/partial diagnostics that could expose an invalidated answer.

## C1: temporal meaning

Extend the auditor's typed temporal assessment with a documented-state scope and a consistent assertion category: source observation, retrieved comparison, present-world assertion or none. Historical source observations may quote current/active language without becoming present-world assertions; malformed or contradictory category/scope pairs fail. Missing categories retain conservative legacy handling. Require the auditor to distinguish: a historical source observation; the newest observation among the supplied dated records; and a claim of currently active real-world state. A documented-state assertion must identify its comparison scope and supporting sources, consider supplied newer/conflicting relevant records, and avoid claiming the archive is exhaustive. Semantic approval does not bypass numeric/reference checks.

Remove the bare current/latest keyword veto as the decision rule. Preserve conservative handling for actual current-world assertions and unknown scopes. Return an explicit retrieval-limited current-state summary when all delivered assertions are historical or documented-state observations. Do not change `current_state` into a fabricated proof of active coverage. Prompts and acceptance must agree that “latest documented terms found” can answer the user's question while cancellation/replacement remains unresolved.

- Temporal assessment distinguishes source-relative recency from real-world truth for policies, account balances, medication records and contracts, without special-casing their nouns.
- Historical coverage uses indexed subject/type/date signals and measured budgets. It must work when the query domain is unknown and must expose truncation/coverage limits.
