# Reader and verifier model capability comparison

Status: proposed next diagnostic after the reconciled failed source-record comparison;
independent Spec/design and Standards plan reviews cleared the acceptance contract.
Diagnostic runner implementation is in progress; no native admission under this plan.

## Decision to test

Prior controlled full-source reading and omission-recovery comparisons did not
preserve all required meanings or eliminate false verifier approvals. Source-record
reading recovered two previously missing qualifications in the first real case,
but ten completed pairs retain 34/37 required meanings in each final arm, with two
refund-negative losses offsetting the replacement gains. Three unsupported statements
survive into final answers. Two other pairs timed out. Both independent grades fail.
These results do not justify production activation or another prompt-only rollout.

Test the model-capability hypothesis already declared in the query-reliability plan.
Use the established full-original reader interface and independent source-only audit
with two existing routes. Do not add another agent, semantic filter, retrieval stage,
source-record compiler or production configuration switch in this diagnostic.

The control route is gemini-3.8-flash through the existing LiteLLM Google Gemini route.
The proposed comparison route is paperless-brain-deep-review, currently configured
as gpt-5.6-sol with no custom API base. Route existence is verified metadata, not
proof of availability, quality, price or compatibility. Before private execution,
verify the destination and exact request serialization, and obtain specific transfer
authorization if required. Do not repurpose the prior Gemini-only approval.

## Fixed inputs and three observations per route

Preserve all six original development documents and both frozen B2 repetitions,
including the unchanged questions, requirements, source-date policy, complete
citation windows, both primary-reading variants, gold and previous result/grade identities.
Create a new private package. No old result is rerun, resumed, regraded or overwritten.

Create one fixed baseline observation inventory per document by concatenating its
two unchanged B2 primaries in their original order, then a separately frozen challenge
appendix. Preserve every occurrence, text and reference; retain a sidecar origin for
each assigned audit unit. The appendix includes all newly confirmed factual failure
patterns from the reconciled source-record grades and explicit supported counterparts,
plus the observed source-value/date representation controls. Review each appendix
label against original text before admission. Preserve the original gold unchanged;
new labels live in their own admitted file. Quarantine the disputed subtotal wording
judgment as uncertain rather than assigning a convenient supported/unsupported label.

This maps qualifier broadening, arithmetic composition/allocation, N/A-field overreach,
and information-request versus coverage-election errors to fixed claims. Missing
refund receipt-negation stays a required reader-retention control in the original gold.
Any pattern not actually admitted must be reported as untested. No native input gets
the label, prior verdict, grading explanation or appendix-family name.

For each of three predeclared trials per document, each model route performs:

1. Audit the identical fixed baseline observation inventory. This isolates verifier
   capability on the same claims, including already known false inputs. Neither audit receives prior
   reading notes, other model verdicts or evaluator gold.
2. Read the complete original through the existing document-local corrected reader
   interface. The original question is supplied before any answer candidate. Preserve
   the reader's raw initial response and its existing bounded protocol correction.
   Score every initial and corrected output. A content-free structural correction
   may establish transport/protocol compatibility, but cannot erase an initial
   semantic defect. An uninterpretable initial response leaves its semantic outcome
   unresolved and prevents a clean capability pass for that route/trial. No semantic
   correction is requested or supplied, and there is no favorable-attempt selection.
3. Audit every observation in that route's new reading against the same full original,
   with empty prior reading notes. Preserve every occurrence and reference, original
   audit ledger, subset re-audit and final answer. Do not repair or compose new prose.

This is one model-route variable within the new matched comparison. Baseline audit
results measure verifier capability independently of fresh-reader content. Fresh
reading retention and finalization measure the combined route. The diagnostic does
not isolate the cause of every internal model behavior, establish archive completeness,
or qualify the complete query pipeline.

There are eighteen matched document/trial pairs and thirty-six route executions:
six documents, three trials, two routes. Trial one uses forward document order,
control then comparison route, and baseline audit before reader/fresh audit. Trial
two reverses document and route order and executes reader/fresh audit before baseline.
Trial three rotates forward document order by three places, alternates the first
route by document index, and uses baseline then reader/fresh audit. The baseline
inventory itself is unchanged across all trials. All scheduled trials execute even
after a semantic failure; no favorable outcome selects another attempt.

Routes and stages execute sequentially; existing audit batches may use the same
four-call concurrency within an owned stage. An isolated document/route execution
failure marks its remaining stages not run and continues later fixed controls;
shared integrity failure or cancellation stops the entire run after owned cleanup.
Application/proxy cache bypass does not prove upstream provider independence. Record
reported cache usage and any dependency evidence. Three executions are not described
as three independent samples unless that independence is established.

## Resource contract to review after local measurement

Use the same explicit per-call timeout for both routes in this new comparison.
Propose 180 seconds per call and 1,800 seconds per audit stage (including its
owned batches and subset work), with unchanged no-output-token-cap, SDK retries disabled,
agent retries disabled, cache bypass, audit protocol correction and subset policy.
The former 90-second failures remain failed measurements. A larger bound does not
establish their cause or repair production timeout behavior, and timing comparisons
against earlier runs must state the changed resource condition.

Measure complete actual pinned-SDK localhost MockTransport bodies for both routes,
all known baseline audit requests and full-original reader requests. Conditional
reader corrections, fresh-reading audits and subset audits must be captured and
accounted even when their native bodies depend on model output. Provider capacity,
monetary cost and conditional request counts remain unknown until measured.

Only then select exact logical/case/aggregate call and elapsed ceilings. Every
attempt, correction, failure and subset call consumes the same inherited budgets.
Budget exhaustion is an explicit incomplete result; no source or observation can
be dropped to fit. Every route must join all owned work before another route can
change model configuration. No persistent app config or GitOps state changes.

## Acceptance and next decision

Freeze code, runtime profiles, route destinations, exact input buffers, gold,
schedule, preflight and budgets with two independent admission receipts before any
private native dispatch. Validate complete known request sets before opening clients,
and compare actual known SDK bodies at dispatch. Retain raw terminal/protocol/semantic
outcomes and final decisions under an exclusive output directory.

Two independent graders examine all assertions and references, every initial and
subset audit decision and final delivery, as well as unchanged required meaning.
Report raw false approvals separately from scalar/date rejections that happened to
block them. A correct factual rejection must not be relabeled a false rejection
because another claim in the same occurrence is valid. Unobserved work remains
unobserved, never an inferred semantic pass or a fabricated unsupported claim.

A route cannot pass by abstaining on all facts, omitting required meaning, laundering
an earlier false approval through subset filtering, or completing fewer controls.
For the model-capability hypothesis, require all scheduled executions, no false raw
semantic approval of any independently graded assertion in fixed or fresh audits,
no wrong raw rejection of any independently supported assertion in either audit,
zero unsupported assertions in fresh readings (including extras), and retention of
every unchanged required gold meaning. Preserve uncertain judgments as unresolved;
do not force labels or count them as clean passes. Also require a measurable improvement over a
failing matched control; equal results do not establish an advantage. This is only
eligibility for a subsequent design decision, never a complete-pipeline or release pass.

Independently grade final source/scalar/temporal delivery and record all guard failures.
Semantic support, source attribution/precision and deterministic delivery are
separate recorded dimensions. The frozen source-year and event-role requirements
remain mandatory in control labels and all fresh output grades. In particular,
unsupported expansion of a two-digit year is not a supported positive or a model
semantic pass; field association must establish the exact event and century.
Known deterministic representation losses remain blockers to query qualification,
but are not misreported as evidence about which model made the better raw judgment.
This avoids an experiment whose model-capability gate is impossible solely because
a frozen, unrelated representation guard is already known to reject a valid field.

If the comparison route improves semantic judgments but source-value guards still
reject valid facts, design a general source-bound value representation separately;
do not loosen currency/date matching while false relationships survive in the raw
auditor. If neither route meets the criteria, record the rejection and revise the
model-capability hypothesis using the observed failures. No production release,
held-out pass or whole-query completeness claim follows from this development test.

After a measured semantic pass, the existing complete-design, global evidence and
counterevidence, fresh held-out, full-query, visual browser and immutable GitOps
release gates remain required.

## Implementation and measured admission preparation

The diagnostic uses the existing full-original corrected reader and source-only
finalizer through isolated, sequential model profiles. It captures every raw SDK
body, initial/corrected response, audit ledger and final delivery. Complete frozen
preflight validation checks both models, all reader and fixed audit bodies and the
synthetic fresh-audit stages before opening native clients. Native dispatch compares
known request bodies to that preflight. Input bytes are verified and retained before
awaiting work; exact code/runtime, schedule, limits and two receipts bind admission.

The initial localhost measurement captured 228 requests over 36 route executions:
72 reader requests (including every content-free correction), 120 fixed baseline
audit requests and 36 synthetic fresh-audit requests. Largest body: 76,317 bytes;
total: 6,808,368 bytes. These synthetic counts do not predict native fresh-reader or
subset-audit demand. The largest fixed baseline has eight initial audit batches.

Select maximum 128 attempts and 3,600 seconds per route execution, 1,536 attempts
and 14,400 seconds aggregate. Each call is bounded at 180 seconds; each audit stage
at 1,800 seconds, inside its remaining route/aggregate deadline. The aggregate caps
are shared by both models and all corrections, subsets and failures. Hitting them
produces explicit unobserved results, never selective retries or omitted claims.
Capacity, monetary cost and provider independence remain unverified. The next exact
package review must bind the final preflight and these ceilings before execution.

Review reproduced and closed three implementation defects before admission: legacy
native-call cleanup could receive a second cancellation at overlapping deadlines;
the configured finalizer timeout did not itself bound the complete multi-wave/subset
audit stage; and origin sidecars could lose their provenance fields. Native calls
now join teardown through repeated caller cancellation, each complete diagnostic
audit has one inherited deadline, and every occurrence origin is reconstructed and
compared. Both timeout regressions failed before their fixes. The complete backend
suite passes 1,014 tests with 58 expected skips (49.330 seconds). These checks are
execution-contract evidence, not model accuracy measurements.
