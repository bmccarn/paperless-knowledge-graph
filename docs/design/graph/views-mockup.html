View switcher

Six views of the same data. The five on the left follow the focus entity; Overview shows the whole archive. Focus, filters, selection, the inspector and the legend carry over from view to view.

States

Current view: Focus
Keyboard focus on Timeline
No focus entity, from the map
Announced after a switchTimeline of Policy HM-4471 (auto)

One Tab stop. Left and Right move between views, Home and End jump, Enter or Space switches. Without a focus entity, Focus, Explore, Timeline and Table are unavailable and say "Pick an entity on the map or search first." Paths keeps its own pickers.

Keys and questions

ViewKeyThe question it answers
Focus1nWhat is this entity linked to, and how do those things link to each other?
Explore2What lies two or three steps away, and where do the links loop back?
Timeline3When was each link documented, and what arrived when?
Table4tEvery link of this entity, sorted and filtered.
Paths5pHow are these two entities connected?
Overview6What is in the archive, and which parts hang together?

Number keys follow the switcher order. n, t and p stay from the first graph release. No key acts while a text field has focus.
